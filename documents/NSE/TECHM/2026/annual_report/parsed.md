FY26 performance
We closed FY26 with revenues of ₹ 56,815 crore, 
a 7.2% increase over the previous year. Full-year 
operating profit stood at ₹ 7,152 crore, growing 
39.2% year-on-year, while profit after tax was 
₹ 4,811 crore, up 13.2%. 
Our operating margin expanded from 9.7% to 
12.6%, an improvement of 290 basis points 
over FY25, driven by disciplined execution of 
multiple operational levers. Project Fortius 
remained central to this progress, supported by 
actions across pricing, productivity in fixed-price 
engagements, optimisation of subcontractor 
costs, rotation, and G&A efficiency.  
Deal momentum remained strong. We closed 
the year with total deal wins of USD 3.79 Bn, an 
increase of 42% year-on-year, reflecting continued 
progress on our transformation and growing 
client confidence in our execution capability and 
solution-led approach.
This performance was complemented by strong 
cash discipline. Our focus on working capital 
management and cash conversion remained 
consistent, with free cash flow generation of 
USD 616 Mn, supported by disciplined receivables 
management and operational efficiency. This 
enabled us to maintain a consistent approach to 
capital allocation and shareholder returns. 
Investment continues to be a critical pillar of our 
transformation. Even as we drove productivity 
improvements through a consistent action stream, 
we also invested in strengthening the operating 
foundation of the business. During the year, 
we expanded capabilities across service lines, 
strengthened leadership depth, and invested 
in internal tools, platforms, and learning and 
development. We also augmented capabilities 
across sales, marketing, and key verticals 
to improve go-to-market effectiveness and 
execution quality.
In parallel, we are driving the infusion of 
Generative AI across delivery. This remains a key 
operational priority, and we have made significant 
progress, with Generative AI now embedded in 
approximately 90–95% of engagements across 
our top accounts. We will continue to build on 
this momentum as we scale adoption further 
across the business.
We also made good progress on the integration of 
our portfolio companies, with backend integration 
advancing well during the year. This has further 
strengthened operational alignment and execution 
consistency across the organisation. 
Commitment to shareholders
In line with our stated capital allocation policy, 
the Board approved a final dividend of ₹ 36 per 
share, taking the total dividend for the year to 
₹ 51 per share – the highest in the Company’s 
history. This resulted in a full-year dividend payout 
ratio of 104% of PAT and 91% of free cash flow, 
reflecting confidence in the strength and resilience 
of our cash flows and our continued commitment 
to shareholder returns.
Looking ahead: FY27
FY26 marked the Stabilisation Phase of our 
three-year transformation roadmap. We remained 
focused on disciplined execution and delivered 
steady progress across our core priorities – margin 
expansion, deal momentum, cash generation, 
and continued investment in future capabilities – 
Generative AI and the broader ecosystem including 
LLMs, platforms, and our people. The progress we 
have made has strengthened the resilience of our 
business and positions us well as we move toward 
our FY27 goals. 
As we enter FY27 with a stronger foundation for 
the next phase of our transformation, I would 
like to thank you all for your continued trust and 
support as we move forward on our journey.
